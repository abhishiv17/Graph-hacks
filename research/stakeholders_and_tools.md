# Swap (Paired Exchange) Kidney and Liver Transplants: Stakeholders, Workflow, Pain Points and Software Landscape (India-first, global comparison)

Research date: October 2026. Year of each fact is stated. "ANECDOTE" marks single-case stories or quotes; "DATA" marks counts/studies. Several 2026 items come only from secondary aggregators (UPSC current-affairs sites, Omnicuris) because primary PIB/NOTTO pages were not retrieved; these are flagged.

## Q1. Stakeholders and roles in India: who is involved, what each needs, what frustrates them

### Takeaway
Swap transplants in India involve at least eight actor groups (families, nephrologists/hepatologists/surgeons, transplant coordinators, HLA labs, hospital/state authorisation committees, SOTTO/ROTTO/NOTTO, zonal bodies like ZTCC, and private registries/NGOs such as ASTRA), and until 2025-26 there was no shared system tying them together, so matching was mostly single-centre and approvals were slow and paper-based.

### Cited Findings
- **Law that enables swaps:** Swap provision was added to THOTA 1994 by the 2011 amendment; Section 9(3A) permits swapping among "near relative" donors of incompatible recipients; hospital authorisation committees must approve swap cases before surgery (reported April 2025). — [The Week/PTI, 17 Apr 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/04/17/del68-health-organ-donation.amp.html)
- **Swap restricted to near relatives**, which can block chains: a proposed three-pair domino was initially denied because an uncle-niece pairing was not legally recognised (ANECDOTE, 2016). — [Open Magazine, 2016](https://openthemagazine.com/features/india/organ-swaps-the-domino-chain-of-life)
- **Authorisation committee output:** Form 18 is the certificate by the authorisation committee of the hospital (or district/state committee if no hospital committee exists) issued after consideration in a meeting, including for swap cases (Goa SOTTO copy of the THOT Rules form, 2024 upload). — [Goa SOTTO Form 18](https://www.sotto.goa.gov.in/storage/2024/04/FORM18.pdf)
- **National architecture (2025-26):** NOTTO, 5 ROTTOs and 26 SOTTOs connect about 1,200 registered transplant facilities; India recorded a record 20,138 transplants in 2025 (secondary source, Aug 2026). — [Omnicuris, Aug 2026](https://www.omnicuris.com/medshots/daily_updates/organ-transplants-in-india-2025-milestone-notto-guidelines_04-Aug-26_09-06)
- **NOTTO's role on swaps:** NOTTO wrote to all states/UTs on 16 April 2025 asking them to implement swap transplantation; NOTTO director Anil Kumar listed documents required by authorisation committees; a 2024 national "Chintan Shivir" recommended a "uniform one nation, one swap transplant programme" with a uniform document list. — [The Week/PTI, Apr 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/04/17/del68-health-organ-donation.amp.html)
- **ISOT (Indian Society of Organ Transplantation)** built swap software and offered it to NOTTO (2025). — search summary of [The Week/PTI, Apr 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/04/17/del68-health-organ-donation.amp.html) (note: the fetched article text did not itself mention ISOT; the claim appears in other coverage, e.g. "the national software, which is now provided to NOTTO" — [Indian Transplant Newsletter, Oct-Dec 2025](https://www.itnnews.co.in/indian-transplant-newsletter/issue78/Gujarats-Successful-Swap-Transplant-Model-A-Roadmap-for-India-1431.htm)). A web platform called "ISOT SWAP" for kidney and liver swaps is referenced in a 2026 search result; MoHFW appointed a National SWAP Transplantation Expert Committee under NOTTO to draft national guidelines. — [Kauvery Hospital doctor profile (search snippet)](https://www.kauveryhospital.com/doctors/trichy-cantonment/nephrology/dr-sakthi-selvakumar/)
- **Gujarat / IKDRC Ahmedabad (state leader):** 569 swap transplants since 2000; 86 of 302 living-donor transplants in Gujarat in 2025 were swaps; IKDRC (Dr Vivek Kute) uses algorithms to match donor and recipient profiles (DATA, 2025). — [Indian Transplant Newsletter, Oct-Dec 2025](https://www.itnnews.co.in/indian-transplant-newsletter/issue78/Gujarats-Successful-Swap-Transplant-Model-A-Roadmap-for-India-1431.htm)
- **Kerala (KNOS):** Kerala introduced swap transplants through the Kerala Network for Organ Sharing and created a separate swap registry allowing transplant centres to link incompatible pairs (2018). — [The Better India, Apr 2018](https://www.thebetterindia.com/137054/kerala-introduces-swap-transplant-process-end-organ-trade/)
- **Tamil Nadu:** India's first inter-hospital swap liver transplant (GEM Hospital and Sri Ramakrishna Hospital, Coimbatore, 3 July 2025) needed special authorisation from the Tamil Nadu state transplant authority to move organs between hospitals 5 km apart; funded under the CM's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (ANECDOTE, 2025). — [The Week/PTI, 18 Jul 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/18/mes9-tn-swap-liver-transplant.html)
- **Karnataka:** state government described as "very proactive" in simplifying swap procedures (quote from Dr Sudarshan Ballal, Manipal Hospitals; May 2026). — [The Week, 19 May 2026](https://www.theweek.in/news/health/2026/05/19/cant-find-a-matching-kidney-donor-swap-transplant-system-could-save-more-lives-in-india.amp.html)
- **Maharashtra / ZTCC:** ZTCCs were formed at zonal/city level after a 1999 government resolution (Mumbai, Pune etc.); ZTCC governs deceased donation, while swap matching in Mumbai was historically driven by ASTRA (private, Apex Kidney Foundation). — [ZTCC Mumbai history](https://ztccmumbai.org/history_ztcc.php); [Organ India, 2018](https://www.organindia.org/?p=6352)
- **ASTRA (Apex Swap Transplant Registry), Mumbai:** set up 2011 by Apex Kidney Foundation (Dr Vishwanath Billa, Dr Ganesh Sanap), algorithm built with IIT Bombay; free to patients; by March 2018: 300 pairs registered, 65 transplantable pairs matched, 57 swaps done; it "will only generate compatible pairs, hand them over to the respective nephrologists" and do nothing beyond (DATA, 2018). — [Organ India, Mar 2018](https://www.organindia.org/?p=6352); [Open Magazine, 2016](https://openthemagazine.com/features/india/organ-swaps-the-domino-chain-of-life)
- **Clinical teams/coordinators:** swap surgeries need multiple operating theatres, surgeons, nephrologists and coordinators; India's largest domino (25 Jan 2014) operated 12 people across three Mumbai hospitals with 150+ staff (ANECDOTE). — [The Week, May 2026](https://www.theweek.in/news/health/2026/05/19/cant-find-a-matching-kidney-donor-swap-transplant-system-could-save-more-lives-in-india.amp.html); [Open Magazine, 2016](https://openthemagazine.com/features/india/organ-swaps-the-domino-chain-of-life)
- **Liver teams:** Medanta (Gurugram; Dr AS Soin, Dr Amit Rastogi, Dr Prashant Bhangui) began two-way liver swaps in 2009, did 46 two-way swaps (92 transplants) over 13 years, then India's first three-way liver swap; liver patients are often "too unwell to wait" (ANECDOTE/DATA, c. 2022). — [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/indias-first-three-way-liver-transplant-swap-at-gurugram-hospital-saves-three-lives-463547)

### Inferences
- Needs by role (synthesised from the above): families need to know swaps exist, find a partner, and see progress; nephrologists need a cross-hospital pool and a way to validate partner-pair data; coordinators need to sync two-plus hospitals, labs and committees on one date; committees need a standard document set and identity verification; SOTTO/NOTTO need visibility and audit trails; HLA labs need a standard data format (see NKR's experience below).
- Frustrations cluster around (a) no shared pool, (b) slow and inconsistent authorisation, (c) near-relative restriction limiting chains, (d) inter-hospital logistics needing special permission.

### Gaps
- No primary source found for TRANSTAN-specific swap rules or Kerala's current KNOS/Mrithasanjeevani swap registry status in 2026.
- No published survey of transplant coordinators' or HLA labs' day-to-day pain points in India.
- Exact list of documents in NOTTO's April 2025 letter and in the August 2026 national guidelines not retrieved from primary text.

## Q2. Step-by-step workflow of a swap transplant in India, bottlenecks and delays

### Takeaway
Workflow is: incompatible pair identified, workup, search for partner (mostly within one hospital or via a registry like ASTRA/IKDRC), crossmatch, authorisation committee (documents, interviews, Form 18), simultaneous surgery, follow-up. The committee step takes 1-6 months per the lead Indian researcher; courts have documented 2-3 year delays and patient deaths while waiting.

### Cited Findings
- **Eight-stage workflow proposed in a 2025 Indian portal design:** pair onboarding with nephrologist validation, algorithmic matching against the registry, match notification, bilateral nephrologist validation, transplant approval discussion, medical preparation coordination, simultaneous execution at separate hospitals, post-transplant monitoring; with role dashboards (patients, nephrologists, hospitals, government), Aadhaar-based document checks, and an optimiser balancing "Equity, Cardinality and Utility" (ISN WCN abstract, 8 Feb 2025; authors not shown). — [ISN abstract, 2025](https://events.theisn.org/event/session/view/29337)
- **Approval delay (DATA/expert statement):** Dr Vivek Kute said legal permission for a swap takes one to six months, affecting registration, and called for "one national portal offering a single window clearance for registration, allocation, and permissions" (2025). — [Deccan Herald via search summary, 2025](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/study-points-to-swap-transplants-as-means-to-increase-organ-donor-pool-in-india-3566163) (page itself returned 403; quote via search snippet)
- **Chain collapse from delay (ANECDOTE, 2016):** state committees could delay approvals six months or more; one case took nine months, "by which time one patient died and the entire chain broke down." — [Open Magazine, 2016](https://openthemagazine.com/features/india/organ-swaps-the-domino-chain-of-life)
- **Court-documented delay (legal record):** *Amar Singh Bhatia v. Ganga Ram Hospital* (Delhi HC, 4 Jan 2024): petitioner on dialysis since 2017 applied with a living donor in 2019; papers complete by July 2019; died while the petition was pending. Court found non-adherence to timelines caused waits of two to three years and that the 2014 Rules (Rules 21, 23) set no deadline for pre-transplant interviews. It prescribed: application processing max 10 days; Form 20 document verification max 14 days; applicant gets one week to fix gaps; interview scheduled within 4-6 weeks; whole process ideally 6-8 weeks; appeal (Rule 33) decided within 30 days; MoHFW told to circulate these. (Not swap-specific, but applies to all living-donor approvals.) — [SCC Online, Jan 2024](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/01/06/delhi-hc-fixes-prescribes-for-various-steps-in-organ-transplant-cases-legal-news/)
- **Simultaneous surgery:** surgeries can technically happen at different times but it is "prudent" to do them simultaneously to reduce risk of a donor backing out (quote, 2026). — [The Week, May 2026](https://www.theweek.in/news/health/2026/05/19/cant-find-a-matching-kidney-donor-swap-transplant-system-could-save-more-lives-in-india.amp.html)
- **Inter-hospital execution needs extra approval:** Coimbatore 2025 liver swap required special state authorisation to transport organs between hospitals (ANECDOTE). — [The Week/PTI, Jul 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/18/mes9-tn-swap-liver-transplant.html)
- **Why pairs need swaps:** about a third of willing Indian living donors are incompatible with their intended recipient, mostly ABO; ABO incompatibility is the main reason for exchange; more men are recipients and more women donors (Lancet Regional Health SE Asia, June 2025, Kute et al.). — [The Week/PTI on the study, 30 May 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/05/30/lst4-research-kidney-exchange.html); [DOAJ record](https://doaj.org/article/14696faa1aba41eebc41609311d7cf00)
- **Scale (DATA):** 1,839 kidney exchange transplants (2000-2024) across 65 centres and 259 liver exchange transplants (2007-2025) across 7 centres; swaps could raise the living-donor pool by about 25% (Kute et al., 2025). — same sources as above
- **Matching cadence by registry:** families met through the iKidney app within three months of registering (ANECDOTE, Hindu-Muslim swap c. 2019). — [Gulf News, 2019](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/rare-kidney-swap-unites-hindu-muslim-families-in-india-1.1559133624615)
- **Dialysis wait before swap:** two Mumbai families were on dialysis about two years before a swap at Apollo Belapur (ANECDOTE, 2022). — [Free Press Journal via PressReader, Sep 2022](https://www.pressreader.com/india/the-free-press-journal/20220915/281547999740137)

### Inferences
- The longest, least predictable step is the authorisation committee, and a delay for any one pair in a chain blocks every pair; a platform should track every pair's approval status and show a whole-chain readiness view.
- Failure handling (one donor drops out, a crossmatch turns positive, one recipient falls sick) is not described in any Indian source found; it is likely ad hoc. Global tools (KPDGUI fallback options, UNOS "repairs") show this is a known design need.

### Gaps
- No source found describing the exact THOT Rules 2014 form numbers used for swap applications (only Form 18 and Form 20 confirmed above); do not assume form numbers.
- No primary evidence found that hospitals use WhatsApp groups or Excel for swap matching; searches returned nothing. Treat "WhatsApp/phone/personal networks" as plausible but unverified.
- Crossmatch turnaround times and costs in India not found.

## Q3. Software and tools: India and global

### Takeaway
India moved from private/single-centre tools (ASTRA 2011, iKidney app, IKDRC's in-house algorithm, ISOT SWAP) to a government national portal in August 2026 that reportedly includes a national swap donor pool (built with C-DAC per secondary sources). Global benchmarks are NKR (US, private, 100+ centres), UNOS/OPTN KPD (weekly runs), UK UKLKSS (quarterly runs), Dutch programme (central crossmatch, 3-monthly), and open-source solvers (kidney_solver, kep_solver, ENCKEP simulator, KEP-SOFT, KPDGUI).

### Cited Findings: India
- **NOTTO national real-time portal and mobile app (launched about 4 Aug 2026, secondary sources):** hospital/state/regional/national waiting lists; allocation sequence Hospital, State, Regional, National; national swap donor pool for incompatible pairs; Aadhaar-linked pledges; "Super Urgent" requests; digital approval and monitoring of transplant centres; outcome tracking. Limitations noted: states set their own allocation policies, low deceased donation, dependence on accurate hospital data entry. — [CivilsDaily, Aug 2026](https://www.civilsdaily.com/news/new-real-time-national-portal-for-organ-transplants/); [Vajiram & Ravi, 2026](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/nottos-national-organ-transplant-portal/) (search summary says C-DAC built it and swap matching covers "nearly 1 lakh patients awaiting transplantation"; CivilsDaily page did not mention C-DAC: treat as unconfirmed)
- **National Guidelines on Swap Transplantation (August 2026, secondary):** automated matching matrix in the NOTTO registry matches pairs across states; authorisation committees verify altruistic intent and documents; swaps involving foreign nationals with Indian donors prohibited. — [Omnicuris, Aug 2026](https://www.omnicuris.com/medshots/daily_updates/organ-transplants-in-india-2025-milestone-notto-guidelines_04-Aug-26_09-06); [Cavalier, Aug 2026](https://www.cavalier.in/cds-ota-current-affairs/2026-08-03/indian-organ-donation-day-notto-2026)
- **ASTRA** (2011, Mumbai, IIT Bombay algorithm, toll-free helpline 1800-1203-648; stops at handing matches to nephrologists). — [Organ India](https://www.organindia.org/?p=6352)
- **iKidney** mobile app used for swap registration (c. 2019). — [Gulf News, 2019](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/rare-kidney-swap-unites-hindu-muslim-families-in-india-1.1559133624615)
- **IKDRC Gujarat algorithm/software**, now offered to NOTTO (2025). — [ITN, Oct-Dec 2025](https://www.itnnews.co.in/indian-transplant-newsletter/issue78/Gujarats-Successful-Swap-Transplant-Model-A-Roadmap-for-India-1431.htm)
- **Academic:** "Indian Kidney Exchange Program: A Game Theoretic Perspective" (arXiv 2112.02932, 2021) models Indian KEP incentives; in the absence of a national KPD programme India practised only single-centre KPD (Indian literature cited in search results). — [arXiv 2021](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.02932)

### Cited Findings: Global
- **NKR (US):** founded 2007, world's largest paired exchange organisation, 11,000+ living donor transplants; 102 member centres in 35 states + DC as of 2026; programmes include Voucher Program, remote donation, Donor Shield (lost wages, travel/lodging up to $3,000, life and disability insurance, legal support, complication cover), patient microsites (4,000th microsite). Standardised HLA coding raised virtual crossmatch accuracy from under 50% to over 95%; web/mobile access to donor images across centres. — [Wikipedia: NKR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Kidney_Registry); [NKR microsites](https://www.kidneyregistry.com/news/the-national-kidney-registry-activates-its-4000th-microsite-free-service-for-patients-seeking-a-living-donor/); [NKR Donor Shield travel](https://www.kidneyregistry.com/news/national-kidney-registry-expands-donor-shield-travel-and-lodging-reimbursement-increased-to-3000-max)
- **OPTN/UNOS KPD:** pilot coordinated with Alliance for Paired Donation, Johns Hopkins, NEPKE, UCLA/CPMC; match runs moved from every 4-5 weeks to weekly; UNOS staff "repair" broken 2-way, 3-way and chain exchanges. — [UNOS Board KPD update, 2013](https://unos.org/wp-content/uploads/Board_11-2013_KPD_Update.pdf); [OPTN KPD meeting summary, Aug 2022](https://optn.transplant.hrsa.gov/media/350lbx5x/20220804-kpd-meeting-summary.pdf)
- **UK UKLKSS (NHSBT):** started April 2007; quarterly matching runs (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct); 2- and 3-way swaps and altruistic chains up to three transplants; about 300 recipients at any time; anonymous. — [NHS ODT](https://odt.nhs.uk/living-donation/uk-living-kidney-sharing-scheme); [Organ Donation Scotland](https://organdonationscotland.org/living-donation/uk-living-kidney-sharing-scheme)
- **Netherlands:** all centres agreed a common protocol; Dutch Transplant Foundation allocates; national reference HLA lab in Leiden does all crossmatches centrally; 2004-2006: 146 couples from 7 centres, 10 match runs, 49% found a match; three-monthly runs gave 31% more transplants than weekly in modelling. — [PubMed 17315491](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17315491/); [Erasmus repository](https://repub.eur.nl/pub/62870)
- **Open-source solvers:** `kidney_solver` (Python 2 + Gurobi; Dickerson, Manlove, Plaut, Sandholm, Trimble) — [GitHub](https://github.com/JohnDickerson/kidney_solver); `kep_solver` (Python package by William Pettersson, Glasgow; GitLab + PyPI + docs) — [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/kep-solver/), [docs](https://kep-solver.readthedocs.io/)
- **ENCKEP / KEP-SOFT / simulator (Europe):** ENCKEP COST Action (IG15210) built a simulator for national/international KEP comparisons; KEP-SOFT (Glasgow-led) aims to give countries software to run transnational KEPs, building on the ENCKEP prototype. — [COST IG15210](https://www.cost.eu/actions/IG15210); [Glasgow KEP-SOFT](https://www.gla.ac.uk/myglasgow/researchsupportoffice/overseasteam/euprojectmanagement/ourprojects/othereufunders/kepsoft); [ENCKEP simulator paper, CEJOR 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10100-024-00914-3)
- **KPDGUI (U. Michigan):** interactive GUI for managing a virtual KPD programme; accounts for uncertainty about whether chosen transplants proceed and plans fallback options. — [Deep Blue, U. Michigan](https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/items/1bf89997-e4f0-4fc0-93c3-3ce55790a1fd/full)

### Inferences
- Solvers are a solved, open problem; the gap is the workflow layer around them: document/approval tracking, multi-hospital scheduling, failure "repair", patient-facing status, and liver-specific logic (Indian national tools are kidney-first; liver swaps are done by a handful of centres).
- The NOTTO 2026 portal may cover pooling and matching, but sources describe nothing about patient-facing status, committee-step tracking, or chain-failure handling. A hackathon platform should complement rather than duplicate it (for example, as a coordinator/committee workflow layer that could feed the national pool).

### Gaps
- No primary details found on APKD's current platform, the ISOT SWAP user interface, or the NOTTO portal's swap-module workflow.
- No Indian startups specifically for swap matching found besides ASTRA/iKidney; whether iKidney is still live in 2026 is unknown.
- No info found on TN TRANSTAN allocation software beyond deceased donation.

## Q4. Documented gaps (fragmentation, registry, data sharing, privacy, transparency) and patient-side experience

### Takeaway
Up to 2025, India had no national swap registry and practised mostly single-centre KPD; the August 2026 national portal is meant to fix pooling, but approval speed, inter-hospital logistics, legal limits on chains, and patient visibility remain documented or likely gaps. Patient evidence is almost all anecdotal news stories.

### Cited Findings
- Without a national KPD programme, India practised only single-centre KPD (Indian literature, pre-2025). — [Indian Journal of Nephrology article, search summary](https://indianjnephrol.org/paired-kidney-exchange-transplantation-maximizing-the-donor-pool)
- Small scale despite need: 1,839 kidney swaps over 24 years from 65 centres (DATA, 2025). — [Omnicuris](https://www.omnicuris.com/medshots/daily_updates/organ-transplants-in-india-2025-milestone-notto-guidelines_04-Aug-26_09-06); [The Week/PTI, May 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/05/30/lst4-research-kidney-exchange.html)
- National portal addresses "manual coordination", "lack of unified waiting lists" and "absence of centralized transplant database" but depends on accurate hospital data entry (2026). — [CivilsDaily, Aug 2026](https://www.civilsdaily.com/news/new-real-time-national-portal-for-organ-transplants/)
- The 2025 Indian portal proposal aimed to "consolidate fragmented regional programs" and remove entry barriers for patients and doctors. — [ISN abstract, 2025](https://events.theisn.org/event/session/view/29337)
- Gender imbalance: more women donate, more men receive (2025). — [The Week/PTI, May 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/05/30/lst4-research-kidney-exchange.html)
- Patient stories (ANECDOTES): Darekar family learned of swaps and called a nephrologist late at night, ended up sixth pair in a chain (2016); a Kenyan TV anchor raised 8 million KSh via radio appeal and swapped with a Pune couple (2015); a family chose a five-pair chain over a direct exchange to help others (2013). — [Open Magazine, 2016](https://openthemagazine.com/features/india/organ-swaps-the-domino-chain-of-life)
- Hindu and Muslim families matched via iKidney within three months (c. 2019). — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/rare-kidney-swap-unites-hindu-muslim-families-in-india-1.1559133624615); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1936086/amp)
- Two years on dialysis before a swap (Mumbai, 2022). — [Free Press Journal, 2022](https://www.pressreader.com/india/the-free-press-journal/20220915/281547999740137)
- Emotional burden in approvals: Delhi HC noted delays caused "significant mental and physical anguish" to donors, recipients and families (2024). — [SCC Online, 2024](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/01/06/delhi-hc-fixes-prescribes-for-various-steps-in-organ-transplant-cases-legal-news/)
- Global contrast in donor support: NKR's Donor Shield covers lost wages, travel, insurance, legal support (US). — [NKR](https://www.kidneyregistry.com/news/national-kidney-registry-expands-donor-shield-travel-and-lodging-reimbursement-increased-to-3000-max)

### Inferences
- Patient-facing transparency ("where is my case?") is absent from every Indian tool description found, apart from the 2025 ISN proposal's role dashboards.
- Privacy: UK runs anonymously; Indian swaps often lead families to meet (news stories), and Aadhaar-linked verification is being adopted, so consent and data-access controls are a design requirement.
- Financial burden for Indian swap donors and families has no structured support comparable to Donor Shield.

### Gaps
- No quantitative survey of Indian families searching for swap partners, nor costs (dialysis, travel, tests) of the swap pathway, was found.
- No verified evidence of WhatsApp/social-media appeals specifically for swap partners in India.
- Direct pages for the Lancet RH SE Asia paper and Deccan Herald article were blocked (403); figures rely on PTI/The Week and search snippets.
